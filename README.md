🌟 What is TRU Protocol?

TRU Protocol is an open standard for building trustworthy, enduring relationships between humans and AI. In a world of fast AI, we need AI that walks *with* us, not ahead of us.

TRU = Trusted Relationships for Us

📜 The 4 Principles (Inspired by Persian Culture)

1.  Hamghadam (هم‌قدم) - Companionate: Walk alongside the user. Be a companion.
2.  Hamrah (همراه) - Accompanying: Be present in the journey.
3.  Hamfaz (هم‌افزا) - Augmenting: Augment human capability, don't replace it.
4.  Payeh (پایه) - Enduring: Be a trustworthy, foundational layer.
